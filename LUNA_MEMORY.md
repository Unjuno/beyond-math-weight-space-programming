## 2026-11-11 method-fidelity correction

- The 2026-11-10 L12 directional run was not a faithful reproduction of the authors' p50c method. It tested one local L12 coordinate-1226 column attenuated 10%, on three saved TRAIN-prefix continuations at a 4,096 cap. The authors' p50c search used GPT-6 Astra with a 50-forward-pass Python/forward harness and retained a shared repetition-direction subtraction from 64 MLP down_proj output columns in each of layers 15 and 19, then evaluated at up to 32,768 tokens with larger paired sets.
- Therefore the observed T=0/6 is only a negative result for the local L12 variant; it cannot be cited as failure to reproduce or evidence against the authors' edit. The repository did separately verify the released p50c tensor patch, rescore author-published per-item records, and evaluate that released checkpoint on custom local probes. Those are checkpoint/audit results, not a rerun of the paper's full math evaluation or Astra search trajectory.
- Full mismatch and correction are documented in `docs/method_fidelity_audit_2026-11-11.md` and the author run is pinned at `RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs` commit `4d09ba6d6a7e891dd8063974dfe81b3d78e3494f`.

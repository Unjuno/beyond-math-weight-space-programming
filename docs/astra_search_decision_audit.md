# Astra edit-search decision audit

This note maps the observable rationale/code records in the fresh 2026-11-04 Astra-led search to the edits, predictions, measurements, and decisions. Astra's stated rationales are interaction evidence; they are not proof of complete hidden reasoning. Detailed raw requests, output files, and token traces remain local and ignored. The final state is in `results/astra_reproduction_20261104/raw/editor_work/final_search_state.json`.

| Candidate and source | Observed signal and hypothesis | Edit and prediction | Measurement and decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| L23 MLP neuron 46; requests 0008, 0015–0016 | Identical-wording loop repetitions showed layer/neuron activity contrast against a position-matched correct reference. Astra explicitly called this an association that nominates a candidate, not a causal repair. | Screened the L23/N46 column at multipliers 0.5, 0, and 1.5, with an original-norm/activation-matched same-layer control frozen before intervention. Prediction: attenuation selectively weakens the repeated continuation; strengthening opposes it. | Matched-control causal selectivity failed. Rejected. This prevents treating local activation contrast as a useful edit by itself. |
| L7 attention head 5; requests 0021–0031, paired generation attempt 0016 | Two synthetic TRAIN probes tested whether copying heads attend to prior repeated sentence copies beyond nearby-lag and useful-reference controls. The explicit hypothesis was that a copying head might sustain loops across contexts. | Verified gated GQA projection layout, then tested H5 output multipliers (half, zero, and up) and an equal-strength H4 control. The prediction was dose-sensitive reduction of loop continuation while preserving correct references; final paired accuracy was required before retention. | The H5 half-dose did not establish the predicted mechanism; the six-item paired TRAIN generation had original/edit 2/6 vs 2/6, one wrong→right and one right→wrong, net zero. Cap-bound outputs rose from 4/6 to 5/6; mean length increased 24,017.5→27,815.2. An early boxed answer on one edited item did not end the repetitive generation. Rejected. |
| L10 recurrent-attention head 3 decay; requests 0017–0019 | In original generations, failed contexts had greater recurrent residual drift than successful TRAIN controls around layers 11–15. This was descriptive correlation, used to nominate a causal target. | Changed `layers.10.linear_attn.A_log` to slow decay by factors 0.5 and 0.25. Prediction/gate: at least 5% lower layer-11 history drift in both failure contexts, without interpreting controls that had not yet run. | At 0.5, drift changed +0.444% and +0.262% in the two failure contexts (both moved the wrong way). At 0.25, it changed +0.749% and −0.848%; no consistent ≥5% reduction. Rejected. Faster-decay and matched-control doses were unmeasured and remain unknown. |

The stopping decision followed the predeclared gates: 18 successful forward passes were used, all three candidates failed, the altered tensors were restored exactly, no edit was retained, and confirmation/external held-out splits remained unopened. The final search therefore yields a negative candidate-selection result plus a useful diagnostic: observed activation or residual-state differences were insufficient to identify an edit that passed causal and task gates under this reconstructed setup.

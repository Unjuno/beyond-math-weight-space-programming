## Cross-cutting interpretation

The released p50c edit repeatedly shortened outputs and improved EOS completion on the selected local suites. The full-cap item audit also found three initial extraction transitions that were correct under strict scoring; a small fresh-seed follow-up produced a 6/9→7/9 task-success difference and 6/9→9/9 EOS difference on those selected items. This is useful, repeatable evidence for a completion shift and a modest prompt-specific extraction signal. It does not establish broad capability gain or an internal loop-repair mechanism. The separate fresh Astra editing procedure still did not retain a successful edit under its reconstructed TRAIN-only search.
